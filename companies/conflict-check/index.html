How far has the conflict in your team gone?

Every team has conflict. The question is how far it has escalated, because each stage needs a different response. What 1 honest conversation solves in stage 2 needs a mediator in stage 6.

This check is for team leaders and HR. Think of 1 specific conflict in your team and answer 10 questions about what you see. You get the likely stage on Friedrich Glasl's model of 9 stages, what that stage looks like, what to do this week and who usually solves it.

I use the same model in my team workshops, together with working styles, so teams can name what is happening before it gets personal.

This check is an orientation tool. It shows how a conflict usually escalates, based on Friedrich Glasl's model of 9 stages. Every team is different, so use the result as a starting point for a conversation, not as a label for people.

Glasl's 9 stages in 3 groups

  1. 1 Hardening, 2 Debate. The team can usually solve this alone, with a leader who names the tension and keeps the conversation fair.
  2. 3 Actions, not words, 4 Images and coalitions, 5 Loss of face. This usually needs a structured conversation or a team workshop, led by someone the team trusts and who is not part of the conflict.
  3. 6 Threats, 7 Limited destructive blows, 8 Fragmentation, 9 Together into the abyss. This usually needs HR or outside help, such as a mediator. At this point the people involved can rarely step out of it on their own.
