What I look for in talent

  • Culture fitSomeone who shares your values and will feel at home in your company.
  • Hands-on experiencePeople who have really done the work, not just people with the right words on their CV.
  • Team fitA person your team will enjoy working with, and who makes the whole team stronger.
  • Working styleSomeone whose way of working matches how your team thinks, decides and communicates.
