Workshops for teams

Better teams start with understanding.

Every team works well while things are going well. My workshops are for the moments when they do not, when feedback gets postponed, a small tension turns into silence and good people quietly start looking elsewhere. Together we find out how each person thinks, communicates and reacts under pressure, and we turn that into team rules people actually follow on Monday morning.

What we work on

Every workshop is built around your team and its real situations. These are the topics we go through together, step by step, with exercises instead of lectures.

9 levels of conflict.
Where are we?

Communication and conflict

With Friedrich Glasl's 9 stages of conflict, your team learns to see how far a disagreement has gone. In the first 2 stages people solve it themselves, while from stage 6 on it already needs HR or outside help, which is exactly why timing matters so much.

Personality styles

Based on DISC and Thomas Erikson's 4 colours, each person finds their style. The Driver guards time, the Connector guards energy, the Supporter guards people and the Analyst guards accuracy, and once your team sees this, a lot of everyday friction simply disappears.

Emotional intelligence

Following Daniel Goleman's 4 pillars, we start with the body, because it reacts before we think. Each person maps their own triggers, such as status, control, fairness, belonging and certainty, and learns a pause sentence for the moment the wave of anger rises.

Strengths

With CliftonStrengths each person discovers their top 10 talents, the engine that drives them, and the 5 where they need cover from others. Only about 1 in 33 million people share the same top 5 in the same order, so your team map is truly one of a kind.

Cross-cultural communication

Using Erin Meyer's Culture Map, we look at how directly people speak, how they criticise, whether they disagree with the boss and how they build trust. I add real stories from leading teams from India, the Philippines and Pakistan in Dubai.

What else shapes us

In the end we put the 5 layers together, which are style, strengths, culture, introversion or extroversion, and role and experience. Seeing the whole person explains why the same sentence lands so differently with 2 colleagues.

Does this sound like your team?

  • Feedback and difficult conversations keep getting postponed until "a better moment".
  • The same problem comes back every few weeks, and every time the anger is a little bigger.
  • People talk about each other in the kitchen instead of with each other in the meeting.
  • The quieter people stop sharing ideas, and your best people quietly start looking elsewhere.
  • Different working styles and cultures create friction that nobody names.
  • Everyone agrees in the meeting, and then nothing changes afterwards.

If you nodded at even one of these, you are not alone, because I see it in almost every company. A conflict never stands still, though. Every stage it climbs makes it harder and more expensive to solve, so the best time to work on it is now, while your team can still fix it on its own.

How it works

From our first conversation to the moment your team uses the new rules every day, this is how we do it together.

  1. 1

    We talk

    First we talk, you and me, about what is happening in your team and what success looks like for you.

  2. 2

    Team check

    Your team answers a short anonymous pulse check, so I come prepared and already know where it hurts.

  3. 3

    The workshop

    We meet live or online, phones are on silent and what is said stays in the room. The leader only receives the overall team picture, never individual answers.

  4. 4

    Team map and rules

    Together we build your team map and agree on 3 to 5 rules people will actually follow.

  5. 5

    Your team takes over

    After the workshop, the leader has the team summary and the rules, so your team can keep tracking its own progress.
