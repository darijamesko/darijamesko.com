Which working style are you?

I use this quiz with job seekers and teams, because most problems at work are not about skills. They are about how we come across. A fast person sounds rude to a careful one, and a careful person sounds difficult to a fast one.

Answer 32 short statements about how you usually behave at work. Do not overthink it, the first answer is usually the honest one. At the end you see your main working style, your mix of all 4, how you come across to the other styles, and small things you can try in the next 2 weeks.

There are 4 styles: Driver (red), Connector (yellow), Supporter (green) and Analyst (blue). Nobody is only 1 colour, and no style is better than another.

The 4 working styles at a glance

Driver (red)

The one who gets things moving. You are brave, fast and you say the thing everyone else is avoiding.

Connector (yellow)

The one who brings the energy. You bring warmth, ideas and the ability to get people excited about something before it even exists.

Supporter (green)

The one everybody trusts. You are steady, you listen properly and you make people feel safe enough to be honest.

Analyst (blue)

The one who keeps the quality high. You think carefully, you check properly and you keep the standard high when everyone else is rushing.
