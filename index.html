Workshops

Every team works well while things are going well. My workshops are for the moments when they do not, when feedback gets postponed and small tensions slowly grow into conflict. With working style and CliftonStrengths profiles, the 9 stages of conflict, a formula for difficult conversations and team rules written by the team itself, your people learn to fix things early, before they cost you time, energy and your best people.
