Hi, I’m Darija.

I’m the founder of Thrive Through HR, a career coach and HR consultant with 12+ years of experience across different sectors and HR.

I’ve built my experience in Slovenia, Germany and the UAE (Dubai), and today I support job seekers, companies and teams across Europe, the UAE and the wider Middle East.

My Mission

  • I believe work can feel good. Through my workshops, I help people understand themselves and each other, communicate better and handle conflicts. When people know their strengths and feel confident, the whole team benefits.
  • I help people recognise their own value and build confidence in their careers. Through honest one-on-one conversations, personality tests and self-reflection, we discover their strengths, find their voice and explore new career opportunities.
  • I help companies find people who fit the role, the team and the business. I take time to understand the company, its culture and its people, because a great hire should make sense for everyone.
